Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Core Philosophy: 100% Offline

SpendSathi was built on a single, fundamental premise: Your financial data belongs to you. Our application is 100% offline. We do not have servers, we do not require user accounts, and we do not sync your data to the cloud.

2. SMS Parsing & Local Storage

SpendSathi requires SMS read permissions solely to parse transactional messages (like UPI or bank alerts) and automatically categorize your expenses.

  • No Data Leaves Your Device: The SMS parsing happens entirely locally on your smartphone.
  • Local Database: All parsed expenses, custom categories, and application settings are stored in an encrypted local database on your device.
  • No External Transmission: SpendSathi does not upload, share, sell, or transmit your transaction history or SMS data to us or any third-party services.

3. No Trackers or Analytics

We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking software. Because the app is completely offline, it does not track your usage habits or phone diagnostics.

4. Permissions Required

To function correctly, SpendSathi requests the following permissions:

  • READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS: Used exclusively to detect bank and UPI transaction messages in real-time to automate your expense tracking.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the way SpendSathi operates, please contact the developer via the feedback form on our homepage or directly at: mohi.varma18@gmail.com.
