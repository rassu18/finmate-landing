Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Local Financial & SMS Processing

SpendSathi is designed to protect your financial privacy. Transaction amounts, merchant names, account balances, and SMS text are parsed and stored 100% locally on your Android device—they are never uploaded to any SpendSathi server. Our SMS filter strictly reads bank, credit card, and UPI transactional alerts and completely ignores OTPs and personal messages.

2. Permissions We Request

  • READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS: Strictly used to read incoming bank and UPI transactional alerts to automatically log expenses locally. OTPs and personal chats are ignored, and SMS content is never uploaded to external servers.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows SpendSathi to send local alerts such as daily spending summaries, budget warnings, or categorization reminders.
  • INTERNET: Used only for Google AdMob ad delivery on the Free tier and Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics app diagnostics (matching our Google Play Data Safety disclosure).

3. Free Tier Ads & Diagnostics

While your financial and SMS data never leaves your phone, the ad-supported Free tier uses third-party Google SDKs that make standard network requests for ad delivery and app stability:

  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymous usage events, device model/OS version, and crash diagnostics to improve app stability.
  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements on the Free tier and may use advertising IDs and generalized location signals per Google's policies. Upgrading to SpendSathi Pro removes ads.

These third-party providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information. We recommend reviewing the Google Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Security

Because your transaction data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data relies on your device's security measures. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device's operating system updated.
