What SMS Do We Read?

SpendSathi filters strictly for bank, credit card, and UPI transactional alerts and completely ignores OTPs and personal messages. All SMS parsing happens locally on your Android device.

Free Tier Ads & Diagnostics

Your financial & SMS data is never uploaded to any SpendSathi server. The ad-supported Free tier uses Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics for ad delivery and crash diagnostics (matching Google Play Data Safety); Pro (₹149 one-time) removes ads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about SpendSathi pricing, privacy, backups, and Indian bank support.

Is SpendSathi free?

Yes. SpendSathi is ₹0 / Free Forever for all core expense tracking features, including automatic on-device bank & UPI SMS parsing, Spend Score, safe daily spend, monthly budgets, donut & trend charts, manual JSON/CSV backup & restore, and PDF financial statements (ad-supported). You can unlock the optional ₹149 one-time Ad-Free / Pro upgrade inside the app.

Does SpendSathi upload my SMS or bank data to a server?

No. Transaction amounts, merchant names, balances, and SMS text are parsed and stored 100% locally on your Android phone—never uploaded to any SpendSathi server. We strictly ignore OTPs and personal chats. The Free tier uses Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics for ad delivery and app stability.

Which Android phones and Indian banks are supported?

SpendSathi runs on Android 8.0+ smartphones (No iOS) across Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Redmi, Realme, Vivo, Oppo, Google Pixel, and Motorola, and automatically parses transaction SMS alerts from major Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak Mahindra, Yes Bank, IndusInd, PNB, Bank of Baroda) as well as UPI transactions via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.

What happens to my data if I change or reset my phone?

Because your transaction history is stored locally on your device without any cloud account, export a manual local backup file (JSON/CSV) from Settings → Backup & Restore before resetting or switching devices, then import that file on your new Android phone.
