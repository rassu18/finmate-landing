Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about SpendSathi pricing, privacy, and Indian bank support.

Is SpendSathi free?

Yes. SpendSathi is ₹0 / Free Forever for all core expense tracking features, including automatic on-device bank & UPI SMS parsing, Spend Score, safe daily spend, monthly budgets, donut & trend charts, and PDF financial statements (ad-supported). If you prefer zero ads plus backup & restore, you can unlock the optional one-time Ad-Free / Pro upgrade in INR inside the app.

Does SpendSathi upload my SMS or bank data to a server?

No. All SMS parsing happens 100% locally on your Android phone. SpendSathi never uploads your SMS messages, bank balances, or transaction history to any server, and never asks for your netbanking login or UPI PIN.

Which Android phones and Indian banks are supported?

SpendSathi runs on Android 8.0+ smartphones (Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Redmi, Realme, Vivo, Oppo, Google Pixel, Motorola) and automatically parses transaction SMS alerts from major Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak Mahindra, Yes Bank, IndusInd, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Federal Bank) as well as UPI transactions from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.
