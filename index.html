What SMS Do We Read?

SpendSathi filters strictly for bank, credit card, and UPI transactional alerts (e.g., debits, credits, and UPI merchant payments) to log your expenses automatically.

We completely ignore OTPs, promotional messages, and personal SMS chats. Your SMS text is parsed locally on your phone and is never transmitted to any SpendSathi server.

Privacy & Data Safety Transparency

Financial & SMS Data: 100% stored and processed locally on your device—transaction amounts, merchant names, balances, and SMS text are never uploaded to our servers.

Free Tier Ads & Diagnostics: Matching our Google Play Data Safety disclosure, the ad-supported Free tier uses Google AdMob (ad delivery) and Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics (app stability). Upgrading to Pro (₹149 one-time) removes ads.
